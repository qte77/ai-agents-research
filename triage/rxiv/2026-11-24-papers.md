### GRACE: Parallel Best-of-N Multi-Agent Analysis of Closed-Form Questions over Dataframes with Guardrail Selection and Adaptive Retry

### Experience Rot: Experienced Agents Fail Where Novices Succeed

- arxiv: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20922>

- findings: experienced agents fail on most tasks after a silent change; experience can be detrimental when documentation is incomplete; simple remedies like announcing updates or pointing to documentation are insufficient

### When Should Decision Models Compare Candidates?

- arxiv: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20335>
- summary: The study compares three decision models - Pointwise scoring, Setwise scoring, and Candidate Attention - to determine whether scoring candidates independently or allowing their scores to depend on other candidates is more effective.
- methods: comparative analysis, controlled synthetic tasks, relational training
- findings: Setwise scoring performs better on some tasks; Candidate Attention does not consistently improve upon Pointwise scoring; Setwise models learn candidate dependencies with targeted relational training
