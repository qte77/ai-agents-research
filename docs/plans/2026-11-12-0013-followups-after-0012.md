that repo and follows that repo's own CLAUDE.md/CONTRIBUTING.

**Watch-outs:**

- Stale `GH_TOKEN`/`GITHUB_TOKEN` variables in this devcontainer shadow the stored `gh` credential; unset
  them for `gh`/git network calls only, never for commits (the commit signer needs the token).
- `main` requires CodeFactor on an up-to-date head: after each merge the next PR is behind — update it and
  wait for CodeFactor again. CodeFactor occasionally sticks on "pending"; closing and reopening the PR
  re-triggers it.
- The link check is not a required check. A failure on a link the PR didn't touch is rechecked once and
  then left alone (no lychee excludes). Known one: coldtea.ai's AGENTS.md field study (503 since
  2026-11-12).
- Subagents' web-fetch reports sometimes arrive in the lead's inbox instead of the subagent's; relay them.
- Research claims that something is absent ("no MCP", "no source") need a site-wide check — two such
  claims were wrong in plan 0012's batches.
